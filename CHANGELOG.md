# Changelog

All notable changes to Gin Rummy are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added
- First release of Gin Rummy: A Club Car Card Game for Windows, a native game written in Go, set in the club car of the Starlight Limited as it crosses the prairie at night.
- Full Gin Rummy rules: ten cards each and a first upcard; sets and runs with aces low; knock with 10 or less deadwood; the other player lays off on your melds; undercuts score 25, gin 25 and big gin (all eleven cards) 31. Dead hands are dealt again when the stock runs down to two cards. Play to 100, 50 or 150, with the game bonus, a box bonus for every hand won and double score for a shutout.
- Your hand sorts itself into its melds with a brass bracket over each one, and your deadwood count is always showing. Pick the card to knock with and the knock card goes face down.
- Single player against a different passenger every game, each with their own portrait, a name and title that fit their face (Grandma Edna, Chef Gus, Captain Ned, Mrs. Vanderhoff, Maestro Lou, Professor Abernathy and many more), their own way of talking and their own way of playing: some knock the moment they can, some hold out for gin.
- Play Together on the same computer: two people take turns, with a "pass the computer" screen so neither sees the other's cards.
- Play Together online: host a table or join a friend's by address, on your home network or over the internet. If your friend leaves, a passenger takes over their cards.
- A hand-drawn railroad deck: the dining-car chef, the conductor, the engineer and the cattle baron for kings; the torch singer, the heiress, the mystery lady and the schoolteacher for queens; the porter, the newsboy, the brakeman and the bartender for jacks, each in front of a train window on the night prairie. The card back has a streamliner and the Starlight star.
- A green baize club-car table with a brass rail, a cup of dining-car coffee and a score pad.
- Three original tunes: "Starlight Swing" (clarinet, piano, walking bass and brushes), "Prairie Moon" (a harmonica waltz over a guitar) and "Boogie on the Rails" (boogie-woogie piano over a train-rhythm shuffle), with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for shuffling, dealing, drawing, taking the discard, discarding, knocking, gin (the train bell), big gin and dead hands (the steam whistle), undercuts, laying off and scoring.
- Choice of pace: Easy, Regular or Quick.
- Saved record: games played and won, gins, knocks, undercuts, shutouts, winning streaks and your biggest win.
- Credits & Changelog screen, How to Play guide, and the version number on the table and in the title bar.
