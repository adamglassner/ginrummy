GIN RUMMY - A Club Car Card Game  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
==============================================

Open Gin Rummy from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. Or just double-click GinRummy.exe.

HOW TO PLAY
- Two players. Each gets 10 cards; the next card turns up to start the
  discard pile.
- Make melds: a set is 3 or 4 of a kind, a run is 3 or more in a row of
  one suit (aces are low). Cards not in a meld are deadwood: aces count
  1, number cards their number, faces 10.
- On your turn draw one card, from the stock or the top of the discards,
  then discard one.
- When your deadwood is 10 or less you may knock. The other player lays
  off what they can on your melds. You score the difference in deadwood,
  but if theirs is the same or lower, they undercut you: the difference
  plus 25.
- Gin (no deadwood at all) scores the other player's deadwood plus 25,
  and they can't lay off. Big gin (all eleven cards) is plus 31.
- The game ends at 100 (or 50, or 150). The winner gets 100 more, and
  everyone gets 25 for every hand they won. A shutout doubles it.
- F2 = new game, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen, Esc = close.

WAYS TO PLAY
- Deal: you against a passenger in the club car, a new one every game.
- Play Together > Same Computer: two people share this PC. Before each
  turn the game asks you to pass the computer over, and cards turn face
  down in between.
- Play Together > Online: one person picks Host, the other picks Join and
  types the host's address (shown on the host's screen). Online games use
  port 47250; across the internet the host may need to forward that port
  on their router.

Your record and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\GinRummy, outside
the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by Ironite.
